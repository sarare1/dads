Hyperparameters
Reciprocal Points (RPL / ARPL)
How strongly the auxiliary RPL open-set objective shapes the shared embedding space during training, relative to the primary classifier + triplet losses. Higher lets RPL converge faster but risks pulling the embedding space away from what the primary threshold method wants.
Adds a generator network that adversarially synthesizes "confusing" embeddings targeting each known class, trained alongside the classifier in a min-max game — the classifier learns to keep rejecting them as open-space even as the generator tries to make them pass. Tightens RPL's decision boundaries beyond the passive open-space margin loss alone, at the cost of a less stable, sometimes slower-converging training run. Off by default (plain RPL) — turn on for a genuine RPL-vs-ARPL comparison across two training runs.
