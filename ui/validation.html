Test Dataset
Testing evaluates against the model's own class taxonomy (RADAR_FAMILY_00–19) with ground-truth labels — different from the Dataset page's interleaved CSV, whose labels don't correspond to this classifier at all. Generate a classification dataset on the Dataset page first (that's also what Training trains on), then select it below — or edit/ replace its rows and re-upload your own version.
Held-Out Test Evaluation
The Training page's accuracy/AUROC come from the validation split baked into that training run. Testing draws on either an uploaded dataset (below, once one exists) or a fresh auto-generated sample — a sanity check that the model generalizes, not just fits one lucky split.
